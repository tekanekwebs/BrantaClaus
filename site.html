Meet Our Elf

It took us a long time to find our elf, Mantra Claus. There are a lot of yes-men in the world today but not a lot of men who have the courage to just say "NO!" He's a good man. Since we're not giving anyone anything, he's all we need. Somehow, he finds time to take calls from around the world despite his busy schedule of milk & cookies, cartoons, & nap time. He tells people like it is when they ask us for stuff! BTW, you're not getting anything, either! Now, IT'S YOUR TURN TO GIVE! YOU can join the honorary elf brigade by just helping someone in need with your time and/or $!

Ideas for Generosity Without Spending Money

Those who lack money can give of their time! (I welcome suggestions.)

Here will be video interviews with unsung heroes.

Generosity Ideas

Idea 1
1

Youth Tutoring & Coaching

Tutoring & coaching sports teams for youth.

Volunteer your time after school or on weekends to help kids with reading, math, homework, or basic drills and teamwork on a local field.

Idea 2
2

Sharing Surplus Food & Essentials

Take food & other items to the homeless which you would otherwise throw away. (This could be a first step toward breaking the ice with someone who has few resources or options in life.)

Pack clean, unspoiled leftover food or usable household items you would otherwise discard and offer them respectfully as a simple, human way to start a conversation.

Idea 3
3

Helping With an I.D. Card

Help a homeless person get an I.D. (Oftentimes, the only thing keeping the homeless from employment is an I.D. Card.)

Walk alongside someone through the paperwork, transportation, and local office steps needed to obtain an official I.D. that can unlock job opportunities.

Idea 4
4

Print-List Lending Library

Start a lending library for the homeless. (Because it is sometimes difficult to find an outlet where a phone can be charged, they are not inclined to read books online. Many would enjoy hardcopy books. Rather than turn your car into a bookmobile, print a list of available titles & deliver upon request. Expect that many books will be lost or damaged, but this kind of thing will bring the homeless in touch with something that feels like mainstream society.)

Gather spare physical books, print a simple catalog of what you have on hand, and bring requested titles directly to people while holding losses lightly as part of genuine connection.

Idea 5
5

Mentoring a Prisoner's Child

Take the child of a prisoner to hang out with you & your kid.

Include a child affected by a parent's incarceration in everyday family outings, play, and normal routines so they feel welcomed, supported, and not forgotten.

Idea 6
6

Children's Hospital Clown Visit

Dress up like a clown & visit a children’s hospital. (They nor their parents will ever forget that in their desperate time of need.)

Coordinate with hospital guidelines to bring lightheartedness, silly moments, and warmth to kids and exhausted parents going through a stressful stay.

Idea 7
7

Nursing Home Friendship

Visit a nursing home & get to know one person in need of a friend.

Spend unhurried time listening to stories, chatting, or keeping company with a resident who rarely gets visitors and longs for consistent friendship.

Idea 8
8

Practical Help for Someone With a Disability

Help a disabled person.

Offer steady, practical assistance with errands, household tasks, mobility, or daily routines—always asking first and respecting their independence.

Idea 9
9

Personal Support After Trafficking & Exploitation

Work personally with those coming out of sex trafficking & modern slavery. (Don't just do politics. Remember: Politics is ineffective.)

Partner with trusted local support efforts to show up consistently with personal care, encouragement, and practical help for survivors rebuilding their lives.

Idea 10
10

Walking Alongside Addiction Recovery

Help walk someone out of chemical dependency, alcoholism or other destructive addictions.

Be a steady, accountable presence who listens without judgment, encourages healthy habits, and supports someone day by day on the road to recovery.

Idea 11
11

Prison Visitation

Visit a prisoner.

Follow facility rules and approved programs to offer genuine human conversation, dignity, and encouragement to someone behind bars.

Idea 12
12

Hiring an Ex-Prisoner

Hire an ex-prisoner.

Give someone with a past record a real chance at honest work, training, and a fresh start where effort and character are judged fairly.

Idea 13
13

Teaching English to an Immigrant

Teach English to an immigrant.

Practice everyday conversation, vocabulary, and confidence-building one-on-one so a newcomer can navigate work, school, and community life more easily.
